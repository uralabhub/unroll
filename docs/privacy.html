Unroll privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Unroll copies photos from your camera to your phone. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. Nothing you do in Unroll is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos and videos read from your camera, SD card or Ricoh GR are saved to your photo library. Unroll keeps small previews and file details (EXIF, film recipes) in its own storage so the next connection is faster.
  • Location. If you start a Photo Walk, Unroll records your route on the phone to tag imported photos. If you allow it, Unroll also reads the location of iPhone/Android photos taken at the same time. Routes are never uploaded and you can delete them in the app.
  • Settings and import history, so the same shot is not imported twice.

Connections

Unroll talks only to your camera (USB-C or the camera's own Wi-Fi) and to the App Store / Google Play for purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

Permissions

Photos (to save imports), Location (Photo Walk, optional), Local network (Ricoh GR, optional), USB/camera access. Each is asked for only when you use that feature and can be turned off in system settings.

Children

Unroll does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
