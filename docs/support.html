Unroll support

Email uranashel44@gmail.com with your phone model, camera model and what happened. We reply within two working days.

Camera doesn't show up

Use a USB-C data cable (not charge-only), set the camera's USB mode to MTP/PTP or "USB card reader", and unlock your phone. On iPhone, tap Allow when asked to access the camera.

Imports stop when I leave the app

Keep Unroll open while importing. If iOS or Android pauses it, the import continues when you come back.

Restore Pro

Settings → Unroll Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.
