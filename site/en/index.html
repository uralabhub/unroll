FAQ

Quick questions before you start

For camera USB mode notes and Android USB conflict fixes, check the Support guide.

Which cameras and card readers work with Unroll?

Unroll supports standard SD and microSD readers over USB-C and Lightning across Fujifilm, Sony, Leica, Ricoh GR, Canon, and Nikon bodies. Ricoh GR cameras also support direct transfer over camera Wi-Fi.

Are original files on my SD card ever modified?

No. Memory cards mount strictly read-only, so Unroll only copies your selected photos and never deletes or modifies card originals.

How do non-GPS cameras get map locations?

Start a Photo Walk on your phone when heading out. On import, Unroll matches photo timestamps to the route and corrects clock drift if you snapped a Clock Sync QR frame.

Do you upload photos or require an account?

No. Browsing cards, reading recipes, matching routes, and framing exports happen directly on your phone. See the full Privacy Policy.

Can I restore Unroll Pro if I get a new phone?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google Play account on the same OS and tap Restore purchases inside Settings.
