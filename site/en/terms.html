2. Free tier, 7-day trial, and Unroll Pro

Unroll starts free so you can test it on a real roll before paying anything: you can browse whole cards, filter ★ and 🔒 picks, read film recipes, log Photo Walks, use three frame styles (Gallery, Caption Bar, and Thin with a small corner mark), and import up to 10 photos per day.

Upgrading to Unroll Pro removes the daily cap and corner mark, unlocks all six frame styles, and adds Auto-import Picks plus automatic geotagging. Four plans are offered: Weekly at $1.99 per week, Monthly at $3.99 per month, Yearly at $14.99 per year (with a 7-day free trial for first-time subscribers), and a one-time Founder Lifetime unlock at $29.99. Prices are in US dollars; your store shows the local price before you confirm.

The Weekly, Monthly and Yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period or free trial. Your Apple or Google account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the period. You can manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, and any unused portion of a free trial ends upon purchasing a paid plan.

Because Apple and Google handle billing and receipts directly, uranashel cannot issue refunds on your behalf; please use Apple or Google Play purchase support if an accidental charge occurs. Also note that since Unroll has no separate user accounts, purchases stay tied to the Apple ID (iOS) or Google account (Android) used at checkout and can be restored anytime inside the same platform.

3. Photo ownership and card backups

You keep full ownership of every photograph, recipe record, and framed export created with Unroll. Nothing is licensed back to us.

Memory cards open strictly read-only so Unroll cannot delete camera files, and optional Android geotagging writes standard APP1 GPS data solely to saved JPEGs while leaving RAW, HEIF, and video files untouched. Because cables, card readers, and flash media can still fail unexpectedly, always confirm your photos are safely imported and backed up before formatting any card.
